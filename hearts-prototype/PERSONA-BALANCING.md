# Persona balancing, version 2

This is the reading the compass uses. It is stored as persona v2: each band has inclusive scale ranges, a short description, and the doors and talks to lean on. The numbers live in `src/lib/persona-v2.ts` and are seeded onto the persona bands.

Doc A is not the master. Where the three documents disagree, this note says which one was followed and why.

## What each document actually contains

- **Doc A** (`A-hadeeth-bandwidth-scales`) is the only place with numeric ranges. Its persona table is not the ten HEARTS scales. The columns are Discipline, Desires, Ego, Anxiety, Trust, Belonging, Gratitude, Faith, Compassion and Self-Control. Anger and Greed are absent. Trust is not a separate HEARTS scale. Anxiety is the dust side of Worry. Discipline and Self-Control both describe the HEARTS discipline scale, so v2 uses their overlap when both are present.
- **Doc B** (`B-overview-personas-short`) and **Doc C** (`C-overview-personas-full`) are portraits: generation, culture, language, gender, and a worked example. They do not give numbers. Doc C also prints the −10 to +10 ladders for the scales. Where a portrait and Doc A’s box disagree about who a person is, the portrait decides the centre and Doc A’s box is tightened so the bands no longer contain each other.
- **scales-to-jibril.md** chooses the doors. A low scale leans on the remedy door in that note, not on a door picked for the shape of a sentence.

A band matches a reading only when every scale sits inside its range. Two bands are distinct, for that rule, when at least one scale’s ranges do not overlap. v2 is pairwise disjoint on that test. A published persona is never written onto the learner.

## Anger and Greed

None of the three documents gives an Anger row or a Greed row on the persona tables. Doc A’s vulnerabilities and Doc B/C’s portraits are the source of the new rows.

- **Social Activist.** Doc A names burnout, anger, cynicism, and an ego tied to impact, and the healing line is tawakkul and managing anger. scales-to-jibril starts this band at units 37 and 27. Anger is therefore the heated band, −8 to −4, and it does not meet any other band. Doors: W7 (fasting and the tongue), W15 (qadr), W19 (it was Jibril, the teaching after the heat). Talks: patience, and the short piece on anxiety as a way into trust.
- **Secular Muslim.** Doc A puts desires at −6 to −2 and self-control at −6 to 0. Greed is read the same way, −6 to −2, the unchecked pull. scales-to-jibril says mercy before rules, starting at unit 31 or 22, so the doors are W10, W5 and W16.
- **Devout Practitioner.** Doc A’s vulnerability is self-righteousness and judging others, not rage. Anger sits on the held side, 0 to +4. Greed sits mildly held, +1 to +6, beside Doc A’s desires at +2 to +5.
- **Everyone else.** The Anger and Greed windows are inferred from the same portrait: a Traditionalist’s authoritarian tone (Doc B/C) is a cool, tight anger at −3 to −1, not the activist’s heat; a New Muslim’s unsettled belonging is not a greed story, so greed stays near the middle.

## Devout Practitioner and Traditionalist

In Doc A these two boxes are almost the same. Faith is +6 to +9 for Devout and +5 to +9 for Traditionalist. Belonging, gratitude, desires and discipline also pile on top of each other. A containment match could not tell them apart.

Doc B and Doc C do tell them apart. The Devout portrait is cultural practice: family prayer, the home language, the norms of the house. The Traditionalist portrait is Sharia, a literal reading, Arabic in religious settings, and the mosque. That is a difference of fidelity to the inherited wording, not a difference of “more belonging”.

v2 follows B and C for the split, and keeps Doc A’s high ritual numbers where they still fit:

- **Traditionalist faith is 8 to 10.** That is the top of Doc A’s faith box, and it belongs to the band whose portrait is the law and the wording. No other band reaches 8.
- **Devout faith is 4 to 7.** Doc A had +6 to +9. The top of that box is given to the Traditionalist so the ranges no longer contain each other. Devout discipline stays high and exclusive at 7 to 9 (Doc A discipline +6 to +9, tightened). Compassion stays narrow, 0 to 3, which is Doc A’s 0 to +4 with the top left for the mercy-first bands.
- Belonging for both is capped at 7. Family-Centred owns 8 to 10, because Doc A and the portraits put that band’s centre in the household.

## The other eight

Ranges below are the v2 windows. “Doc A” means the likely range in that table, after mapping Anxiety to Worry, Desires to Desire, and Discipline with Self-Control by their overlap.

- **Social Activist.** Compassion 5 to 9 (Doc A +5 to +9). Discipline 2 to 4 (the overlap of Doc A discipline +2 to +6 and self-control +1 to +4). Faith 2 to 6. Anger as above.
- **Secular Muslim.** Faith −8 to −3 and desire −8 to −5 are pulled below Doc A’s −5 to +2 and −6 to −2 so they do not contain Cultural or Progressive. Discipline −6 to −2 is the overlap of Doc A’s two control columns. Doors from scales-to-jibril: mercy before rules.
- **Cultural.** Faith −1 to 2, inside Doc A’s −1 to +3 and below Devout. Compassion 1 to 4, disjoint from Progressive. Desire −2 to 2. The portrait (Doc B/C) is identity, family and Eid, with ritual that comes and goes, so discipline stays −2 to 2.
- **Seeker.** Worry −8 to −4 is the exclusive window. Doc A’s anxiety reaches −4, and the portrait is confusion and isolation, so the open worry is the centre rather than a wide faith. Belonging −3 to 2 stays above the New Muslim’s unsettled band. Doors: W15 and W5.
- **Progressive.** Doc A’s ego note is “pride in being enlightened”, so ego is not forced low: −2 to 2, not the old −8. Compassion 6 to 8 separates this band from Cultural. Faith −2 to 0 is the low edge of Doc A’s −1 to +3. Doors: W5, W16, W10.
- **Academic.** Doc A’s vulnerability is arrogance and a loss of heart, while the numeric ego box was only −3 to +4. v2 pulls ego up to 4 to 7 so the arrogance has its own window and does not sit inside every other band. Discipline 2 to 5 is the overlap of Doc A’s two control columns. Doors: W17 and W16.
- **Family-Centred.** Belonging 8 to 10 is exclusive (Doc A +5 to +9, the top kept here). Gratitude stops at 6 so New Muslim can own 7 to 10. Worry 1 to 5 matches Doc A’s anxiety up to +5, the worry for the people at home. Doors: W15 and W10.
- **New Muslim.** Gratitude 7 to 10 is exclusive (Doc A +3 to +9, the top kept here). Belonging −8 to −4 narrows Doc A’s −5 to +3 to the unsettled end; a settled convert leaves the band, which is what the portrait describes. Faith 4 to 7 overlaps Devout’s faith, and gratitude is what separates them. scales-to-jibril starts this band at units 4, 8 and 2, all inside W2, the sitting. The talk to lean on is the one about mosques, companionship and knowledge.
