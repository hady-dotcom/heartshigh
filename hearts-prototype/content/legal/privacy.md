# Privacy notice

**Draft for adviser review.** This is plain wording for a free UK Islamic learning charity app. It is not legal advice. Leon’s lawyer or adviser must approve it before it is shown as final.

## What we keep, why, who can see it, how long, your choices

HEARTS keeps the least we need to run your learning: your name, email, the portal you joined, your answers, and the choices you make about who may see them. Your Compass scores stay with you. Staff only see a mix when ten or more people sit in the same bucket. You can download your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account.

## Who we are

HEARTS hosts the app. Each portal (a masjid, madrasah or school) is the community that invited you and looks after you. In plain words: the portal decides who joins and who teaches; HEARTS keeps the servers, the films and the locks on the data.

If you have a question about your information, write to the privacy contact named by your portal, or to HEARTS at the address on this page.

## What we keep

- **Your account.** Name, email, role, the access code you used, and when you joined.
- **Your learning.** Answers, workbook notes, study plans, completions, harvest lines, and talks you saved.
- **Your choices.** Who may see an answer (only you, your teacher, or other learners), watch history (off unless you turn it on), Gather photos (only with a consent tick), and night alerts.
- **Compass.** Your own scores stay private to you. Staff see only a mix of ten or more people. You never see a number or a label.
- **Opening taps.** Kept on this phone unless you ask us to keep your place.
- **Watch history.** Off by default. If you turn it on, teachers can see more than “this part was finished”.
- **Gather.** Whether you said you would come, whether you arrived, and photos only when the host has a consent tick.
- **Live questions.** The words you send. Under-18s are shown as “A learner asks”, not by name.
- **Consent records.** Which version you agreed to, and the time. For under-13s, that a grown-up agreed, or that the school collected consent on paper.
- **Help requests.** The page, the device and the time, so we can see what broke.
- **Voice, video and photos you upload as an answer.** Treated as your answer, with the same privacy choice.

We do not ask for a date of birth. For children we store an age band only: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or over.

## Why we keep it

To run the class, to show you the right talks, to let your teacher reply, to keep the circle kind, and to meet our duties under UK GDPR and the ICO Children’s Code. We do not sell data. We do not use your words to advertise.

## Who can see it

- **You** see your own account, answers, Compass and garden.
- **Your teacher** sees progress and answers you chose to share with them.
- **Other learners** see an answer only when you chose “share with other learners”. Under-18s cannot turn that on.
- **Portal admins** see people, codes and shared work in their own portal.
- **The HEARTS master** can view-as a person when they need to fix something, with a reason written down.
- **A grown-up** for an under-13 sees the short privacy summary when they agree for their child.

Compass scores are never shown as a name. Circle answers that stay private never leave you.

## How long we keep it

These periods are the draft retention table (brief D04). They will be confirmed with the adviser.

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your account and learning | While the account is open, then as the delete request asks |
| Watch sessions | 12 months |
| Insight and experiment events | 13 months, or counts only |
| View-as sessions and IP hashes | 90 days |
| Audit log | 2 years |
| Rate-limit rows | 1 day |
| A deactivated portal | 90 days, then the master is asked to wipe or keep |
| A delete request | 14 quiet days, then the wipe, unless you sign in and cancel |

## People who help us run HEARTS

We use other services to host and protect the app. They only get what they need.

- **Railway** — hosts the app and the database.
- **Object storage (S3)** — holds films, voice notes and photos you upload.
- **YouTube** — plays many of the talks (youtube-nocookie where we can).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — a short check on the doors so a bot cannot join for you.
- **Email provider** — sends password resets, guardian consent and other system mail, when that transport is on.
- **AI providers** — used only for drafting help for staff (titles, circle examples, feedback themes). Your words are not used to train their models where the contract says so. A portal can run without AI.

Fonts are served by HEARTS. We do not call Google Fonts.

## Children

UK GDPR treats 13 as the age a child can agree to an online service. The ICO Children’s Code asks us to use high-privacy defaults. For anyone under 18:

- answers are not shared with other learners
- watch history is never shared
- a live question does not show their name
- we do not keep extra insight trails beyond counts
- a Gather photo of them needs the host’s consent tick

Under-13s also need a grown-up. Until that agreement is recorded (by email, or by the school on paper), the child can watch, and any answer stays private and is not shown to teachers.

## Your choices

- Download a copy of your data (Settings, when that door is open).
- Ask us to correct a name or an email.
- Ask us to delete your account. Signing in during the quiet days cancels the request. Under-13s need the grown-up.
- Turn watch history and sharing off at any time.
- Write to your portal’s privacy contact, or to HEARTS.

## How to reach us

Your portal names a privacy contact on this page and in Settings. HEARTS can be reached through Get help, or by the email your portal has been given for the host.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, you can write to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) in the UK. We would rather hear from you first and put it right.
