# How we speak to each other here

**Draft for adviser review.** These are community guidelines, not a contract. They are what “Report a concern” points to.

## In a line

Speak as you would in the masjid. No harm, no selling, no sharing someone else’s words. If you are worried, tell a person.

## Adab

Use people’s names with care. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Do not pile on. A child’s words are never a joke.

## No harm

Do not write what would hurt, frighten or shame someone. Do not share a rumour about a person. If you see a message that suggests someone is not safe, use Report and choose “someone may be at risk”.

## No selling

HEARTS is not a market. Do not advertise a product, a channel or a paid class in the circle, in a live question, or on a Gather photo.

## Other people’s answers

An answer you see in the circle belongs to that person. Do not copy it onto another site. Do not screenshot a child’s words.

## When a concern is raised

You will see “Thank you. A person will look at this.” Staff in your portal read the report. They may hide the post, keep it, or ask the person to write again. Your name is not shown to the person you reported.

If the concern is that someone may be at risk, the portal’s safeguarding lead is told the same day. You still see the help contacts on the help screen.

## Children

Under-18s do not share answers with other learners. Their live questions show as “A learner asks”. A Gather photo of a child needs the host’s tick.

## If you need a person

Use Get help. Something broken goes to HEARTS. A question about your learning goes to your teacher. Something worrying goes to the people who look after safety.
