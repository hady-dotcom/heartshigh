# Terms of use

**Draft for adviser review.** This is plain wording for a free UK Islamic learning charity app. It is not legal advice. Leon’s lawyer or adviser must approve it before it is shown as final.

## In a line

HEARTS is a free place to learn. Be kind. Do not sell. Do not share other people’s answers. The masjid or school that invited you looks after the people; HEARTS looks after the app.

## Who these words are for

These terms are between you and HEARTS, the host of the app. Your portal (the masjid, madrasah or school) decides who may join and who teaches. If you are under 13, a grown-up must agree before the account is fully open.

## What HEARTS is

A free learning app: short films from real talks, questions to sit with, a workbook, and evenings in person. There is no shop and no paywall.

## Your account

You join with an access code from your portal. Keep your password to yourself. On a shared family phone, sign out when you are done. If you forget the password, use the reset page. If you have no email, ask your teacher.

You may ask us to delete the account. We wait fourteen days, and signing in cancels that request.

## What you may do

Watch the talks your portal has opened, answer in your own words, and meet people your portal has invited. Use the circle to speak with adab. Report a concern when something feels wrong.

## What you must not do

- Harm, mock or threaten anyone.
- Sell, advertise or collect money through the app.
- Copy someone else’s answer and pass it off as yours in the circle.
- Share another person’s answer, voice note or photo outside the app.
- Try to open another portal, another person’s workbook, or the staff desk.
- Upload anything unlawful, or anything that shows a child in a way that is not safe.

## When something goes wrong

If a concern is raised, staff in your portal look at it. They may hide a post, mute the circle for a short time, or pause an account. Pausing stops sign-in and keeps the learning. A pause is not a wipe.

If someone may be at risk, the portal’s safeguarding lead is told. Learner-facing words will not say that a post was “flagged”.

## Films and words on the talks

Talks stay the speaker’s words. We show our own captions from the transcript we hold. YouTube may still sit under the player. We do not claim the lecture as ours.

## Ending the service

A portal can be paused. HEARTS can close an account that breaks these terms. Your data is kept or wiped as the privacy notice says.

## Changes

When these words change in a way that matters, we publish a new version and ask you to agree again. The date and version sit at the top of the page.

## Law

These terms are meant to be read under the law of England and Wales. If a court finds one line does not stand, the rest still does.
